Our origin

Strategy and control are one decision.

The Vulpine Group was founded on a simple observation: most organizations separate AI strategy from the controls required to deploy it safely. We exist to close that gap.

Strategy teams identify opportunities. Security teams identify risks. Product teams attempt to reconcile the two after the decisions have already been made. In industries where trust, regulation, and operational resilience matter, that approach no longer works.

Enterprise leaders know AI can improve margin, accelerate delivery, and create competitive advantage. The harder question is how to realize those gains without introducing unacceptable operational, regulatory, or reputational risk. Too often, organizations are forced to choose between moving quickly and maintaining control.

We founded Vulpine because we have spent our careers making those decisions from inside some of the world’s most demanding organizations. We have built the identity platforms, fraud systems, payment products, customer experiences, and governance models that operate at enterprise scale, under constant regulatory scrutiny, and where failure carries real consequences.

Our work begins with strategy, helping clients identify where AI can create measurable business value, but it does not end there. Every recommendation is grounded in a control architecture designed to make AI systems governable, observable, and deployable in production.

We believe strategy is only valuable when it can be executed with confidence.
